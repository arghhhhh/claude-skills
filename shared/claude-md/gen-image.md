## Gen-Image - AI Image Generation & Editing (Gemini / Nano Banana)

When the user wants a one-shot AI-generated or AI-edited image — text-to-image, "remove/change X in this image", or style/product-referenced generation — read `~/.claude/skills/gen-image/SKILL.md`. It wraps Google's Gemini image models (nano banana) in a zero-dependency CLI.

Prefer this over ComfyUI when the ask is a single image or edit with no workflow/model requirements; route to ComfyUI for local models, LoRAs, video, or multi-stage workflows.
